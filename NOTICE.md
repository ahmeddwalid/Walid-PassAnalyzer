## zxcvbn

Password strength estimation is provided by [zxcvbn](https://github.com/dropbox/zxcvbn), distributed under the MIT License. The model is loaded lazily and runs entirely in the browser.

## EFF wordlists

The passphrase wordlists in `public/wordlists/` are derived from the Electronic Frontier Foundation's published lists:

- EFF Large Wordlist: 7,776 words
- EFF Short Wordlist, version 1: 1,296 words

Attribution: Electronic Frontier Foundation, CC BY 3.0 US. Source and license links are included in the JSON metadata and on the in-app Dictionaries page.
